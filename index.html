What does AHOS do?

AHOS is a digital product studio in Beirut, Lebanon. We design and build websites, online stores, mobile apps, custom software and AI automations for founders and businesses in Lebanon, the Gulf, Europe and the US.

How long does it take to build a website or app?

A landing page takes about one to two weeks, a full website or online store two to four weeks, and a mobile app or custom platform usually six to twelve weeks. You get a timeline in writing before work starts.

Do I own the code and design?

Yes. On delivery you own all source code, designs and content. There is no lock-in and no licence you have to keep paying for.

How does pricing work?

Every project gets a fixed quote in writing after a free 30-minute call, with payments split into milestones. There is no hourly billing.

Can you work with clients outside Lebanon?

Yes. Most of our work runs remotely, with clients across Lebanon, the Gulf, Europe and the United States. We work in English, Arabic and French.

What happens after launch?

Every build includes a 30-day warranty. After that, care plans cover hosting, updates, monitoring and a monthly block of improvements.
